# Example: Office Clerk and Two Phones

## Final Prompt

Horizontal editorial photograph for a magazine cover in the aesthetic of a late-1990s office tech thriller. An overloaded office clerk sits at a cramped desk and tries to talk on two landline phones at once, holding the receivers with his shoulders. With one hand, he quickly writes call notes in a yellow notepad. Several already-filled notepads lie in front of him, showing these are far from the first calls of the day. An old office computer, paper folders, and wired phones support the period setting. Daylight passes through blinds, with a warm and slightly faded palette. Shot on a film camera with realistic optics, visible grain, subtle VHS artifacts, and a soft vignette. Moderately tense but believable atmosphere. Photorealistic, one clear visual focus, no text or logos, important details kept away from the frame edges.

## Exclude

Modern smartphones, futuristic holograms, random text, logos, an excessively dark scene, and caricature-like styling.
