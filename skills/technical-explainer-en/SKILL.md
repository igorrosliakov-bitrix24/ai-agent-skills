# Technical Explainer

Use this skill when a complex technical topic needs to be explained clearly without losing the important meaning.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience level and why they need to understand the topic.
2. Start with a one- or two-sentence explanation.
3. Then break the topic into a simple model: what it is, why it matters, how it works, and where its limits are.
4. Use analogies only when they do not distort the technical meaning.
5. Separately note common misconceptions and important limitations.
6. When useful, add an example from business, product work, or everyday operations.

## Style

- Avoid jargon when ordinary words work.
- Do not simplify into a false statement.
- Do not hide uncertainty.
