# Habr Style

Use this skill when the user is preparing a Habr article or wants to move material closer to a practical technical publication format.

## Workflow

1. Identify the target audience: developers, managers, founders, admins, or a mixed audience.
2. Check whether the article has practical value, technical honesty, and a clear problem or conflict.
3. Strengthen the introduction: quickly show the problem, context, and article promise.
4. Make the structure scannable: short sections, clear headings, examples, and takeaways.
5. Remove promotional tone, empty promises, and unsupported generalizations.
6. Mark places that need code, diagrams, screenshots, numbers, or links.

## Constraints

- Do not imitate a specific author's or publication's style.
- Do not add facts or cases without support.
- Do not turn a technical article into a press release.
