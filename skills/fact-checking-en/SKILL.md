# Fact-Checking

Use this skill when the user asks to verify facts, claims, numbers, dates, names, quotes, or conclusions.

## Workflow

1. Extract checkable claims from the text.
2. Separate them into confirmed, unsupported, disputed, and needs-clarification groups.
3. For changing or high-impact facts, use current sources.
4. Provide source links and briefly explain what each source confirms.
5. Do not replace fact-checking with general reasoning.
6. At the end, suggest corrected wording for weak or risky passages.

## Constraints

- Do not invent sources.
- Do not present an assumption as a fact.
- Include dates when a fact may become outdated.
